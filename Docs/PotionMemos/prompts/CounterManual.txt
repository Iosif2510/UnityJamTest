A flat scan of a real, messy working note that a young woman who runs a small apothecary shop scribbled for herself, because she keeps forgetting which bottle and which label ink to use when she hands a medicine to a customer. It is NOT a designed document, NOT a form, NOT a poster, NOT an infographic, NOT a table. Nobody laid it out. Real people's memo sheets are chaotic, and this one is too.
