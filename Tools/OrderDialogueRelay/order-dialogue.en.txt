Receiving lines

- rcv_ok: bottle and ink are both right. They like the bottle or can read the label, and are about to drink.

- rcv_ink: only the ink is wrong. The bottle is fine but they cannot read the writing on the label and are puzzled. They do not ask for a particular ink.

After-drinking lines

Right after drinking. Each line is about 80 to 170 characters and says what just happened to the customer. The four lines are independent possibilities. Do not name the illness, any answer identifier or a score.

- aft_cured: all three ingredients and the ink are right. They feel that they are well and are relieved or grateful in their own manner. No complaint about the ink.

- aft_odd: one ingredient is wrong. They are startled that their hands or body have changed colour. They are not simply cured and have not become a slime. Assume nothing about the bottle or label.
